# Code of conduct

## Our standard

This project exists so that agencies can find better leads, keep their data private and
keep their costs down. That only works if the people using it trust each other and the
people maintaining it.

We expect:

- **Plain language.** Technical arguments are fine. Personal attacks are not.
- **Good faith.** Assume the other person is trying to help. Ask before you assume they
  are wrong.
- **Public work, public credit.** Work on the project belongs to the project.
- **Patience.** A review comment is a question, not a verdict.

We do not accept:

- Harassment of any kind, public or private.
- Sexualised language or imagery.
- Personal or political attacks.
- Publishing another person's private information, including credentials.
- Unwelcome attention of any kind.

## Scope

This applies in the repository, in the issues, in the pull requests, and anywhere
someone is representing the project.

## Reporting

Report a problem to the maintainer privately. Reports are handled confidentially, and
the reporter is not blamed for a good-faith report.

## Enforcement

The maintainer decides what happens after a report, and will usually start with a
private word. Persistent or severe behaviour may lead to a temporary or permanent
ban from the project's spaces.
